What counts as a request?

One request that enters Halyard from the internet. Internal calls between your own functions, KV reads and queue operations are counted separately and shown per route.

What happens if I go over?

Nothing is throttled. The overage appears on the next invoice at the published rate and an alert fires the first time you cross 80% of the included volume.

Can I move between plans mid-month?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the next renewal so you keep what you have already paid for.
